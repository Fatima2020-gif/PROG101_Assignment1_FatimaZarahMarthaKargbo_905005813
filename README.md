# Flood Early Warning and Community Alert System (FEWCAS)

A modular digital tool designed to collect flood data, assess risk levels, and present actionable warnings for communities and emergency responders[span_0](start_span)[span_0](end_span).

## Overview
FEWCAS addresses the lack of timely and organized flood information in flood-prone communities[span_1](start_span)[span_1](end_span). It standardizes community reports and environmental indicators to improve disaster preparedness and emergency coordination[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).

## Key Features
* **Data Collection & Validation:** Gathers location, rainfall status, water level, and community reports while validating inputs for accuracy[span_3](start_span)[span_3](end_span).
* **Transparent Risk Assessment:** Uses rule-based scoring to classify risks into **LOW**, **MODERATE**, **HIGH**, or **CRITICAL** categories[span_4](start_span)[span_4](end_span).
* **Automated Warnings:** Maps calculated risk scores directly to tailored safety instructions and emergency recommendations[span_5](start_span)[span_5](end_span).
* **DrainageWatch Module:** Prioritizes municipal drainage maintenance based on blockages, standing water, and citizen complaints[span_6](start_span)[span_6](end_span).
